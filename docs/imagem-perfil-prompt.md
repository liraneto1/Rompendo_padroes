## Prompt utilizado

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: portrait hero illustration for the right side of ROMPA personal reflection questionnaire landing page. Primary request: an elegant image that immediately communicates a personal profile questionnaire and self-observation. Premium editorial 3D still life: a large upright smoked-glass profile card with a refined abstract human head silhouette in warm brushed gold, accompanied by three slim dark questionnaire cards with simple circular choices and small gold check marks. A fine interrupted circular gold line connects the questionnaire cards with the profile, suggesting personal answers becoming a coherent picture. Deep charcoal green-black background #101311, amber #edb75e and warm ivory details, soft cinematic studio light, tactile matte surfaces, sophisticated restrained composition. Vertical 4:5 composition, main objects centered with generous dark margin on every side so it can be cropped on mobile and inside rounded top corners. Main head and cards in middle 65% of frame, dark quiet bottom 20% for existing HTML overlay. This is an educational self-reflection profile, not a clinical evaluation. No charts, no scores, no percentages, no brains, no medical symbols, no puzzle pieces, no roads, no people walking, no logos, absolutely no words, letters or numbers. Produce a finished high-quality raster illustration, not a webpage screenshot.
